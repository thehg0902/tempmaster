Questions

Straight answers, before you call.

How much does AC installation cost?

It depends on your home's size and the system you choose — and we give you a real price over the phone before anyone books a visit, not a sales pitch at your door. Call (416) 890-8550 and describe your home.

Will you try to sell me a rental contract?

No. Temp Master doesn't do equipment rentals — ever. What you buy, you own, with no lien and no surprise buyout down the road.

My AC just died — how fast can you get here?

We're open 24 hours, so call the moment it happens: (416) 890-8550. As an owner-operated company, you're talking directly to the person handling your job, not a call centre — we answer and get moving right away.

Do you only do AC, or the rest of my HVAC too?

All of it. AC installation and repair is our specialty, and we also handle furnaces, heat pumps, ductwork, smart thermostats, humidifiers, and gas piping — one company for the whole system.
