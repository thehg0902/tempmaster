AC Questions

Asked on almost every call.

Repair or replace — how do I decide?

A good rule: if the unit is 15+ years old and the repair is major, replacement usually wins on cost within a few seasons. If it's newer or the fix is small, repair it. We'll give you the honest math for your specific unit — not a pitch.

Should I switch to a heat pump instead?

Sometimes — heat pumps cool like an AC and heat efficiently in the shoulder seasons, and rebates can offset the higher upfront cost. It depends on your home and your heating setup. Ask when you call; we install both, so we have no reason to push one over the other.

Do you handle rebate paperwork?

Ask when you call — rebate programs change often, and we'll tell you honestly what's available and how it applies to your job.

What brands do you install?

We install trusted, widely available equipment — not locked into pushing one brand regardless of what fits your home. Ask when you call and we'll walk you through the options.
