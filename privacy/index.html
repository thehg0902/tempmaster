Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Temp Master (“Temp Master,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit this website or contact us about our heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) services. We are a local HVAC contractor based at 9608 Yonge St, Richmond Hill, Ontario. We handle personal information in a manner consistent with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where it applies, Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

By using this website or submitting information through it, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website's forms or send us information through it.

1. Information we collect

We keep the information we collect to the minimum needed to respond to you and provide our services. Specifically:

Information you give us

  • Quote and contact requests. When you use our quote request form, we collect the details you enter: your name, your phone number, and the message or description of what you need. You choose what to put in the message field — please do not include sensitive information you would not want stored (for example, financial account numbers or health information; we do not need any of that to help you).
  • Phone calls and messages. If you call the number on this site or message us, we may keep a record of your contact details and the reason for your call so we can follow up and provide service.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider's servers may automatically record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you view, and the date and time of your visit. This is used to keep the site secure and working properly.
  • Cookies and local storage. This website does not set its own advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics. Your browser may store ordinary technical data, and the embedded Google Map described below may set its own cookies under Google's control.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect only for these purposes:

  • To respond to your quote request, question, or call, and to schedule and provide the HVAC services you ask for;
  • To follow up about a job, quote, or service we have discussed with you;
  • To keep records for our own business, tax, and warranty purposes;
  • To keep the website and our systems secure and operating correctly; and
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not use your information to do anything you have not asked us to do. We will not add you to a marketing list or send you promotional messages unless you have clearly asked us to, consistent with CASL.

3. Consent

By submitting the quote form or contacting us, you consent to us collecting and using your information for the purposes described above. You may withdraw your consent at any time (see “Your choices and rights” below), though doing so may mean we cannot provide a quote or service that depends on that information.

4. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers. We may use trusted third parties that help us operate — for example, a website hosting provider and, if enabled, a form-delivery service that transmits your form submission to us. These providers may only process your information to perform services for us, and are expected to protect it.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or a lawful government request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others.
  • Business transfer. If our business is sold or reorganized, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

5. Third-party services on this site

  • Google Maps. Our contact area embeds a Google Map so you can find us. When it loads, Google may collect data and set cookies under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram. We link to our Instagram profile. If you follow that link, Instagram's (Meta's) own privacy practices apply once you leave our site.
  • Form delivery. If a third-party form service is used to deliver your quote request to us, its privacy policy will govern how it processes that message in transit. We will name that provider here if and when it is in use.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy — typically for as long as needed to respond to you, provide and stand behind our work, and meet our legal, tax, and warranty record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. How we protect information

We use reasonable physical, organizational, and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information to protect it against loss, theft, and unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Ask what we hold. Request access to the personal information we have about you;
  • Correct it. Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Withdraw consent. Ask us to stop contacting you or to delete your information, subject to legal and record-keeping limits; and
  • Opt out of follow-up. Tell us you would prefer we not follow up, and we will honour that.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details in the “Contact us” section. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children's privacy

This website and our services are intended for adults arranging HVAC work for their home or business. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Links to other websites

Our website may link to third-party websites (for example, Google or Instagram). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be posted on this page.
